Torah — Numbers 20:22 - 21:9

LEB translation

Numbers 20:22 - 21:9

20:22 Aaron Dies And they set out from Kadesh. The Israelites, the whole community, came to Mount Hor.
20:23 Yahweh said to Moses and to Aaron on Mount Hor, on the boundary of the land of Edom, saying,
20:24 “Let Aaron be gathered to his people; he will not come into the land that I have given to the Israelites because you rebelled against my word at the waters of Meribah.
20:25 Take Aaron and Eleazar his son, and take them up Mount Hor.
20:26 Strip off Aaron’s garments, and put them on Eleazar his son; Aaron will be gathered to his people, and he will die there.”
20:27 So Moses did just as Yahweh commanded, and they went up to Mount Hor before the eyes of all the community.
20:28 And Moses stripped off Aaron’s garments and put them on Eleazar his son. Aaron died there on the top of the mountain; and Moses and Eleazar went down from the mountain.
20:29 All the community saw that Aaron died; so all the house of Israel wept for Aaron thirty days.
21:1 Arad Captured The Canaanite king of Arad, who was dwelling in the Negev, heard that Israel came along the way of Atharim; he fought against Israel and took some of them captive.
21:2 Israel made a vow to Yahweh, and they said, “If you will surely give this people into our hand, then we will destroy their cities.”
21:3 Yahweh heard the voice of Israel; he gave to them the Canaanites, and they destroyed them and their cities. They called the name of the place Hormah.
21:4 They set out from Mount Hor by the way of the Red Sea to go around the land of Edom; but the people became impatient along the way.
21:5 The people spoke against God and against Moses, “Why have you brought us from Egypt to die in the desert? There is no food and no water, and our hearts detest this miserable food.”
21:6 The Bronze Serpent And Yahweh sent among the people poisonous snakes; they bit the people, and many people from Israel died.
21:7 The people came to Moses and said, “We have sinned because we have spoken against Yahweh and against you. Pray to Yahweh and let him remove the snakes from among us.” So Moses prayed for the people.
21:8 And Yahweh said to Moses, “Make for yourself a snake and place it on a pole. When anyone is bitten and looks at it, that person will live.”
21:9 So Moses made a snake of bronze, and he placed it on the pole; whenever a snake bit someone, and that person looked at the snake of bronze, he lived.

NIRV translation

Numbers 20:22 - 21:9

20:22 Aaron Dies The whole community of Israel started out from Kadesh. They arrived at Mount Hor.
20:23 It was near the border of Edom. There the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron. He said,
20:24 “Aaron will join the members of his family who have already died. He will not enter the land I am giving to the people of Israel. Both of you refused to obey my command. You did it at the waters of Meribah.
20:25 “So get Aaron and his son Eleazar. Take them up Mount Hor.
20:26 Take Aaron’s official robes off him. Put them on his son Eleazar. Aaron will die on Mount Hor. He will join the members of his family who have already died.”
20:27 Moses did just as the Lord had commanded. The three men went up Mount Hor while the whole community was watching.
20:28 Moses took Aaron’s official robes off him. He put them on Aaron’s son Eleazar. And Aaron died there on top of the mountain. Then Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain.
20:29 The whole community found out that Aaron had died. So the entire nation of Israel sobbed over him for 30 days.
21:1 Israel Destroys Arad The Canaanite king of the city of Arad lived in the Negev Desert. He heard that Israel was coming along the road to Atharim. So he attacked the people of Israel. He captured some of them.
21:2 Then Israel made a promise to the Lord. They said, “Hand these people over to us. If you do, we will set their cities apart to you in a special way to be destroyed.”
21:3 The Lord gave Israel what they asked for. He handed the Canaanites over to them. Israel completely destroyed them. They also destroyed their towns. So that place was named Hormah.
21:4 Moses Makes a Bronze Snake The people of Israel traveled from Mount Hor along the way to the Red Sea. They wanted to go around Edom. But they grew tired on the way.
21:5 So they spoke against God. They also spoke against Moses. They said to them, “Why have you brought us up out of Egypt? Do you want us to die here in the desert? We don’t have any bread! We don’t have any water! And we hate this awful food!”
21:6 Then the Lord sent poisonous snakes among the people of Israel. The snakes bit them. Many of the people died.
21:7 The others came to Moses. They said, “We sinned when we spoke against the Lord and against you. Pray that the Lord will take the snakes away from us.” So Moses prayed for the people.
21:8 The Lord said to Moses, “Make a snake. Put it up on a pole. Then anyone who is bitten can look at it and remain alive.”
21:9 So Moses made a bronze snake. He put it up on a pole. Then anyone who was bitten by a snake and looked at the bronze snake remained alive.

Gospel — Luke 20:9-19

LEB translation

Luke 20:9-19

20:9 The Parable of the Tenant Farmers in the Vineyard And he began to tell the people this parable: “A man planted a vineyard, and leased it to tenant farmers, and went on a journey for a long time.
20:10 And at the proper time he sent a slave to the tenant farmers, so that they would give him some of the fruit of the vineyard. But the tenant farmers sent him away empty-handed after beating him.
20:11 And he proceeded to send another slave, but they beat and dishonored that one also, and sent him away empty-handed.
20:12 And he proceeded to send a third, but they wounded and threw out this one also.
20:13 So the owner of the vineyard said, ‘What should I do? I will send my beloved son; perhaps they will respect him.’
20:14 But when the tenant farmers saw him, they began to reason with one another, saying, ‘This is the heir. Let us kill him so that the inheritance will become ours!’
20:15 And they threw him out of the vineyard and killed him. What then will the owner of the vineyard do to them?
20:16 He will come and destroy those tenant farmers and give the vineyard to others.” And when they heard this, they said, “May this never happen!”
20:17 But he looked intently at them and said, “What then is this that is written: ‘The stone which the builders rejected, this has become the cornerstone.’
20:18 Everyone who falls on that stone will be broken to pieces, and the one on whom it falls—it will crush him!”
20:19 And the scribes and the chief priests sought to lay their hands on him at that same hour, and they were afraid of the people, for they knew that he had told this parable with reference to them.

NIRV translation

Luke 20:9-19

20:9 The Story of the Renters Jesus went on to tell the people a story. “A man planted a vineyard,” he said. “He rented it out to some farmers. Then he went away for a long time.
20:10 “At harvest time he sent a servant to the renters. They were supposed to give him some of the fruit of the vineyard. But the renters beat the servant. Then they sent him away with nothing.
20:11 So the man sent another servant. They beat that one and treated him badly. They also sent him away with nothing.
20:12 The man sent a third servant. The renters wounded him and threw him out.
20:13 “Then the owner of the vineyard said, ‘What should I do? I have a son, and I love him. I will send him. Maybe they will respect him.’
20:14 “But when the renters saw the son, they talked the matter over. ‘This is the one who will receive all the owner’s property someday,’ they said. ‘Let’s kill him. Then everything will be ours.’
20:15 So they threw him out of the vineyard. And they killed him. “What will the owner of the vineyard do to the renters?
20:16 He will come and kill them. He will give the vineyard to others.” When the people heard this, they said, “We hope this never happens!”
20:17 Jesus looked right at them and said, “Here is something I want you to explain the meaning of. It is written, “‘The stone the builders didn’t accept has become the most important stone of all.’ ()
20:18 Everyone who falls on that stone will be broken to pieces. But the stone will crush anyone it falls on.”
20:19 The teachers of the law and the chief priests looked for a way to arrest Jesus at once. They knew he had told that story against them. But they were afraid of the people.
